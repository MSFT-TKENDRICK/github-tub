Two improvements make it easier to review pull requests in the “Files changed” page:
You can save your whitespace visibility preference across pull requests
New and deleted files now use the full width in split view
Save your whitespace visibility preference 
Open Settings → Whitespace changes and choose Always show , Always hide , or Hide for this diff only . Your saved preference is personal and applies across pull requests.
When whitespace is hidden, an active Hide whitespace filter appears beside All commits . Select its X to temporarily show whitespace without changing your saved preference. File counts and diff statistics reflect the displayed diff.
Review new and deleted files in split view 
In split view, new and deleted files now span both columns, as they do in unified view. This gives you more room to review new files and reduces line wrapping.
