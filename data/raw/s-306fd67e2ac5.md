New customer portal help.github.com
The support portal has been redesigned and moved to a new home at help.github.com . It brings support, docs, learning, community, and account resources together in one place with Copilot-powered search across all of them.
What’s new for you:
Find support, entitlements, and self-serve resources from a single entry point with less clicking across separate sites. 
Search anything and get Copilot-powered answers, no sign-in required. 
Easily view GitHub product statuses and important announcements. 
Visit help.github.com to try it out! Please note that we’re gradually rolling this out over the next couple of weeks so you may not see the new experience right away. If you don’t have access yet, support.github.com will continue to work as expected.
Feedback or suggestions for improvement? Feel free to post directly to this discussion in the GitHub Community for consideration.
